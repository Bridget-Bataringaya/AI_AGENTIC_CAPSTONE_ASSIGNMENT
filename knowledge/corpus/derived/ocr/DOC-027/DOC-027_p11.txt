Payment of the provider and Monitoring of contract performance
The Beneficiary Entity shall pay the provider for the supplies and/or services
rendered in a timely manner in accordance with the agreed terms.
The Beneficiary Entity shall continuously monitor contract performance
against the agreed obligations.
Monitoring shall include the review of reports, meetings with providers, and
documentation of performance against the contract management plan.
Amendments, variations, and change orders
All amendments, variations, and change orders to the issued call-off-order
shall be carried out in accordance with the law. The variations shall not
affect the functional specifications in the national framework agreements.
Each amendment shall be documented, justified, and form part of the
contract record.

Where the requirement has specialized technical specifications,

quality, improved delivery terms, or superior overall value compared
to the national framework arrangement; or
Where the procurement is subject to specific legal obligations,
development partner conditions requirements that are incompatible
with the national framework arrangement terms or procedures.
