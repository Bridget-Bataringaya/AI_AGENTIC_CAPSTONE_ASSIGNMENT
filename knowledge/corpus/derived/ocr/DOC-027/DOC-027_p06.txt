PART Ill PROCUREMENT PLANNING
Preparation of the Annual Procurement Plan
All Procuring and Disposing Entities shall prepare and submit the annual
procurement plans to the Secretary to Treasury and a copy to the Authority.
The Authority shall consolidate the procurement requirements from the
different Entities that can be procured through aggregation and inform
Ministry of Finance.
The lead entity shall conduct market research on an annual basis for the
selected procurement requirements for aggregation.
The lead entity in consultation with the participating entity shall be
responsible for provision of technical specifications.
