Providers shall where applicable purchase the bidding documents from the
Lead Entity upon payment of the prescribed fee.
The bidding documents shall require bidders to submit a sufficient number
of bids including the original to be submitted to the Lead Entity. For a Lead
Entity enrolled on the e-GP system, submission of bids shall be done on the
e-GP system.
The bidding document shall incorporate Environmental, Social and Health
(ESHS) aspects and define the minimum scoring criteria for ESHS aspects
relevant to the requirement, ensuring compliance is quantifiable.
Bidding Period
The bidding period shall commence on the next preceding date after
publication of the invitation and end on the deadline for submission of bids.
The minimum bidding period for procurement of supplies and/or services
shall be as prescribed under the PPDA Regulations.
Pre-Bid Meetings
The Lead Entity may hold a pre-bid meeting on the date and time specified
in the bidding document/notice.
Where holding a pre-bid meeting is required, it shall be held in the first % of
the bidding period and the date of the meeting shall be included in the
bidding document to provide sufficient time to enable the bidder to
participate or attend.
The minutes of a pre-bid meeting shall be submitted to the bidders who
accessed the bidding documents or who were individually invited to bid.
Clarification
The requests for clarification shall be handled by the Lead Entity in
accordance with the PPDA Act, Cap. 205 and applicable PPDA
Regulations.
A request for clarifications by bidders and responses to the queries shall be
submitted before or during the pre-bid meeting or within the time stated in
the bidding document before the deadline for submission of bids.
Responses by the Lead Entity to requests for clarifications shall be done
prior to expiry of the bidding period.
