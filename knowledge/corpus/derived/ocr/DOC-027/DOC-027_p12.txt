All exceptions under 24.1 above shall require:
i. Written justification clearly stating:
a) The specific exception being invoked;
b) Detailed circumstances necessitating the exception;
c) Evidence supporting the exception (market survey, provider
performance records, technical analysis, etc.);
d) How the alternative procurement shall achieve value for
money;
ii. Approval by the Authority.

Administrative convenience, preference for established providers, or
reluctance to use Framework procedures;
Unfamiliarity with national framework arrangement terms or

Unwillingness to coordinate with Lead Entity or national framework
providers;
General dissatisfaction with national framework providers without
specific, documented evidence of poor performance or breach of
contract;
Desire to favor specific providers, or service providers not included in
the national framework arrangement;
Failure to plan procurement activities in a timely manner (except in
genuine emergencies beyond the Beneficiary Entity's reasonable
control);
Perception that alternative procurement is "easier" or "faster" without
comparative analysis demonstrating actual benefits.
The Beneficiary Entity shall maintain complete records of all exceptions
invoked, including justifications, approvals, procurement outcomes, and
evidence demonstrating that the exception achieved the intended benefits.
The Authority may, upon review of an exception require additional
justification, supporting documentation, or clarification. The Authority shall
thereafter approve or reject the exception (with or without conditions) and,
where necessary, require the Beneficiary Entity to utilize the national
framework arrangement;
Where the Authority finds that an exception was improperly invoked or
approval was granted without adequate justification, the Authority may
direct the Beneficiary Entity to utilize the national framework arrangement
for future similar requirements and recommend corrective measures.
