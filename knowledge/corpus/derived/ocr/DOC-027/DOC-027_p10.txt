In case of dissatisfaction with the Lead Entity's decision, the aggrieved
bidder shall review the decision and forward their complaint to the PPDA
Appeals Tribunal in accordance with the provisions of the PPDA Act Cap
205 and the Regulations.
Where the complaint is submitted to the PPDA Appeals Tribunal, the
process shall remain halted until determination by the Tribunal.
Attorney General's Clearance of Agreement
Upon expiry of the Best Evaluated Bidder Notice, where there is no
administrative review, the national framework agreement shall be submitted
by the Lead Entity to the Attorney General for clearance before signature of
the contract.
Once the national framework agreement has been approved by the Attorney
General, the call-off orders of the Beneficiary Entities shall not require
subsequent approval.
Signing National Framework Agreements
Upon approval by the Attorney General, the Lead Entity shall submit the
framework agreement to the selected providers for signature.
Where provider fails to sign the framework agreement, the Lead Entity shall
take appropriate action, which may include awarding to the next ranked
bidder, re-tendering, cancellation of the procurement process, and
recommending such a provider to the Authority for suspension.
The Beneficiary Entities shall issue call-off orders to the providers under the
signed national framework agreement without undergoing a fresh
procurement process. Copies of the call-off orders issued shall be submitted
to the Lead Entity.
PART VI CONTRACT MANAGEMENT
Nomination of Contract Managers or Teams
Each Beneficiary Entity shall nominate and approve a Contract Manager or
Contract Management Team responsible for ensuring that contract
execution of the call off order is carried out in accordance with the terms
and conditions of the contract.
Preparation and approval of contract management plans
The Contract Manager or Team shall prepare a Contract Management Plan
using the prescribed template setting out the responsibilities, timelines, and
performance standards of the contract.
