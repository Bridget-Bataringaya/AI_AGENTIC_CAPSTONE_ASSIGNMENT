Attorney General's Clearance of Contract
Upon expiry of the Best Evaluated Bidder Notice and where there is no
administrative review and Attorney General clearance required, the
procuring and disposing entity shall proceed to electronically or digitally sign
the contract.
Where Attorney General's clearance of the contract is required, the
procuring and disposing entity shall obtain approval before the contract
signature.
Signing Contracts
Contract signing shall only proceed after confirmation of availability of funds
or confirmation of funding by the Secretary to Treasury for multi-year
contracts.
Where a provider fails to sign the contract within three days, the procuring
and disposing entity shall take appropriate action, which may include
awarding to the next ranked bidder, re-tendering, cancellation of the
procurement process, or recommending suspension of the provider.
PART VI - CONTRACT MANAGEMENT
Nomination of Contract Managers or Teams
For each contract, the User Department shall nominate for approval of the
Accounting Officer, a Contract Manager or Contract Management Team
responsible for ensuring that contract execution is carried out in accordance
with the terms and conditions of the contract.
The Contract Manager or Team may include members from multiple
entities, and may co-opt additional members or firms as necessary
depending on the complexity of the contract.
Preparation and Approval of Contract Management Plans
The Contract Manager or Team shall prepare a contract management plan
on the System setting out the responsibilities, timelines, milestones and
performance standards of the contract and get the necessary approvals.
