Complaint to the PPDA Appeals Tribunal
Where the complaint is forwarded to the PPDA Appeals Tribunal, the
process that had been halted under Clause 28.2 shall remain halted and
the appeals tribunal registrar shall be notified. PPDA Tribunal proceedings
shall be offline but the decision shall be uploaded on the System.
PART Vill - DISPOSAL OF PUBLIC ASSETS
Nomination of Board of Survey
The Accounting Officer shall nominate a Board of Survey or user
department to identify the public assets to be disposed of. The Board of
Survey shall be comprised of persons from the same entity or outside the
Entity.
Preparation and Approval of Annual Disposal Plans
The PDU shall upload the validated disposal plan in the accepted format.
The appropriate method of disposal under the PPDA Act shall be used for
disposal process.
Amendment of the Disposal Plans
The disposal plan may be amended by the Accounting Officer where
necessary and the amendment shall require an updated Board of Survey
report that shall be uploaded on the System.
PART IX - MISCELLANEOUS
Limitation of Providers from Participating in a Process Under the e-
GP System
Providers suspended by the Authority or debarred by an international
agency of which Uganda is a member shall not participate in the
procurement or disposal proceedings on the e-GP System during the period
of suspension.
