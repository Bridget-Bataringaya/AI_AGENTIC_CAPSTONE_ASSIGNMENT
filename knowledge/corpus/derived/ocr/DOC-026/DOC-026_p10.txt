Prequalification for a group of contracts shall not be undertaken by PDEs.
The shortlist for the restricted methods of procurement shall be developed

A minimum of six bidders shall be shortlisted under the Quotation and
Restricted Bidding methods of procurement and three bidders under micro
procurement. For avoidance of doubt, PDEs can shortlist more than six
bidders or three bidders respectively. Where an Entity is unable to shortlist
the minimum, approval shall be sought from the Contracts Committee to
approve a shortlist of less than the minimum under the quotation and
restricted bidding methods.
Providers shall have access to the bidding documents published on the
e-GP System.
The requirement for a bidder to request and pay for the bidding document
shall apply for procurements and disposals undertaken using the e-GP
System. Bidding fees shall be standardised on the System as UGX 100,000
(One Hundred Thousand Uganda Shillings).
