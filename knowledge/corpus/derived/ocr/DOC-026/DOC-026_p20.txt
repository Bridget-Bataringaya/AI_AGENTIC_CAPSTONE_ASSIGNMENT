The Ministry shall maintain a central register of all exceptions granted under
this clause and periodically analyze exception trends to identify systemic
gaps in e-GP System. The exception data shall be used to inform
improvements to the existing design of the System.
All procuring and disposing entities shall report exceptions invoked under
this clause in their reports to the Authority. The Authority may require
additional reporting or conduct audits of exceptions to ensure compliance
with this Guideline.
Procurement undertaken off the e-GP System without invoking a valid
exception or without obtaining required approvals may be subject to
sanctions under the PPDA Act and Regulations.
Preservation of Electronic Records
The responsible Entity shall preserve the electronic records processed by
the e-GP System for a period not less than seven years from the fulfillment
of the obligations between the parties.
Revocation of Guideline No. 1 of 2020 and Transition
PPDA (Electronic Government Procurement) Guideline No. 1 of 2020 is
revoked.
This guideline shall not apply to procurements or disposals where bids were
advertised or invited but had not been received prior to the commencement
date of this guideline.
