The requirement of obtaining physical proof of receipt of the addendum by
a bidder shall not apply to procurements and disposals conducted on the e-
GP System.
Bidders shall receive an email from the System on amendments to the
bidding document while other providers shall have access to view the
amendments on the bid notice.
Where an addendum is issued and less than one third of the bidding period
remains after issuance of the addendum, the PDE shall extend the deadline
for submission of bids which should be within the maximum bidding period
under Clause 9.2.
Submission of Bids
Bidders shall prepare bids and submit them within the time specified in the
bidding documents.
Submission of bids shall be done by an authorized representative of the
bidder on the System.
Any modification to the bid submitted in the e-GP System shall be permitted
provided it is undertaken prior to the bid submission deadline.
Bidders shall be permitted to withdraw or replace their bids prior to the
submission deadline through the e-GP System. Bidders who withdraw their
bids after the deadline of bid submission shall forfeit their bid securities.
Bidders shall submit bids electronically in compliance with prescribed
formats and within stated deadlines.
Bidders with invalid requirements will not be allowed proceed with bid
submissions.
All bids shall be encrypted upon submission and automatically decrypt at
bid opening.
Bids submitted online shall be scanned for malware by the System before
being accepted onto the System.
A bidder shall instantly be notified where the bid is rejected due to threats
of malware.
