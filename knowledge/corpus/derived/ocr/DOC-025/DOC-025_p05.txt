The performance insurance bond shall be an on-demand insurance bond issued

The format of the performance security in the form of an insurance bond is

Environmental, Social, Health and Safety Performance Security
In addition to the standard performance security, an Environmental Social Health
and Safety (ESHS) performance security shall be required where the ESHS risks
are high as advised by social and/or environmental specialists.
The ESHS performance security may take the form of a bank guarantee or an
insurance bond.
The amount of the ESHS performance security shall be stated in the Special
Conditions of Contract by the Procuring and Disposing Entity and shall be in the
range of one percent (1 %) to two percent (2%) of the contract price.
The sum of the standard performance security and the ESHS performance
security shall not exceed five percent (5%) of the contract price.
The format of the ESHS performance security is provided in the Standard
Bidding Documents issued by the Authority.
Advance Payment Security
Where an advance payment is to be made, an advance payment security shall be
required in accordance with Regulation 43 (2) of the Public Procurement and Disposal
of Public Assets (Contracts) Regulations, 2023, Regulations 43 (1) (d) (iii), 44 (c) (iv)
and 45 (d) (ii) of the Public Procurement and Disposal of Public Assets (Rules and
Methods for Procurement of Supplies, Works and Non -Consultancy Services)
Regulations, 2023.

Advance Payment Guarantee
Where applicable, advance payment security in the form of a Bank Guarantee
shall be required for procurement of supplies, works, non-consultancy services
and consultancy services under the open domestic and international bidding
methods.
The format of the advance payment security is provided in the Standard Bidding
Documents issued by the Authority.
